For local edge networks running open-weights models, define the "Resilience Index" (RI) as the ratio of successful operations to total operations under network scarcity. RI = (Successful Operations / Total Operations) * 100%.

Define these in terms of hardware and network components. For example, scalability could be measured as the increase in operations per second (OPS) with the addition of new hardware. Redundancy could be quantified as the number of duplicate components.

Break down the transition into subsystems (storage, processing, networking, access control) and analyze quantitative constraints and interactions. For instance, define the "Storage Efficiency Index" (SEI) as the ratio of data stored to storage capacity used.
