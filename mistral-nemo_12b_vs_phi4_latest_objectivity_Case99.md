**Long-term Implications of Widespread Sovereign Debt Accumulation**

1. **Global Economy:**
   - **Debt Trap:** High debt levels can lead to a debt trap, where countries struggle to service their debts, leading to reduced economic growth and increased poverty.
   - **Fiscal Space:** Limited fiscal space for governments to respond to economic shocks, such as recessions or crises.
   - **Interest Rates:** Higher debt levels can lead to higher interest rates, increasing borrowing costs for governments and individuals alike.
   - **Currency Devaluation:** High debt can lead to currency devaluation, making imports more expensive and exports cheaper, impacting trade balances.

2. **Macroeconomic Stability:**
   - **Inflation:** High debt levels can lead to inflation, eroding purchasing power. Conversely, low debt levels can lead to deflation, making it harder for economies to recover from recessions.
   - **Economic Growth:** High debt levels can hinder economic growth due to reduced investment and consumption.
   - **Financial Instability:** High debt levels can increase the risk of financial crises, as seen in the 2008 global financial crisis.

3. **Purchasing Power:**
   - **Wealth Inequality:** High debt levels can exacerbate wealth inequality, as those with access to credit can accumulate wealth, while those without are left behind.
   - **Poverty Trap:** High debt levels can trap individuals and households in poverty, as they struggle to service their debts.
