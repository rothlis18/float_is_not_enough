**Edge Network Resilience:**

\[ R = N \times (1 - \frac{F}{N}) \times B \]

Where:
- \( N \) is the total number of nodes.
- \( F \) is the number of failed nodes.
- \( B \) is the remaining bandwidth after blockades.

3. **Network Bandwidth (NB):** NB is the total bandwidth available for data transfer, calculated as \( NB = H \times B \), where \( B \) is the bandwidth per unit.
